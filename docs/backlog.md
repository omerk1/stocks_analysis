16. **First diagonal real-chart review found and fixed a real bug**: a clean, visually obvious 3-touch descending trendline on AAPL was being generated correctly but then discarded — `generate_diagonal_candidates`'s dedup only checked pivot overlap, not slope, so it got dropped as a "duplicate" of unrelated, longer, ascending lines that merely happened to pass near the same points. The similarity threshold reused for this (and for the same check in `lifecycle.py`'s diagonal merge rule) was also two orders of magnitude too loose to ever meaningfully distinguish ascending from descending slopes. Fixed with a dedicated, shared `slopes_are_similar()`. Also raised `diagonal_max_candidates` from 30 to 300 — sorting survivors by raw pivot count before that cap was independently burying short, tight, recent lines under long, low-precision multi-year ones (255 genuinely distinct candidates existed on AAPL after correct dedup; the cap let only the 30 with the most pivots through). AAPL's descending-diagonal count went 1 -> 30, T's went 5 -> 78. 153 tests passing.
17. **Second diagonal review round found and fixed two more real bugs**: (a) T's top-N was still dominated by 8+ genuinely-distinct-but-visually-similar ascending lines because dedup only ever compared candidates that shared pivots — replaced with an actual price-proximity check reusing `--dedup-threshold`; T's total diagonal count dropped 177 -> 132 (effect weaker than hoped, not fully understood — see design notes). (b) More serious: user-reported "floating markers"/zones not starting from a real bar traced to `lifecycle._absorb` displaying merged-in events against the *wrong* geometry — a real T line had touch/break events whose actual close price sat up to 49.6% away from its own ~0.5%-wide band. Fixed by re-classifying events from scratch against the survivor's kept geometry on every diagonal merge instead of unioning stale ones; max deviation dropped to 12.6%, and that residual traces to a genuine pre-existing bad data point (see below), not a logic bug. 154 tests passing. Detection is slower again (~25s on T's long_term window) from the extra re-classification work — not optimized.
18. **Fixed `duration_density`'s bias toward long-history diagonal lines** (found while investigating "why is it only ever surfacing multi-year trendlines"): it normalized span against the *full detection window* (8 years for long_term) regardless of line kind, crushing a genuinely strong, recent ~1-year PAAS trendline (touch_quality 0.37, resilience 1.0, role_reversal 1.0) to duration_density=0.089 purely for not spanning 8 years. Diagonal lines now normalize against a fixed 1-year reference instead. PAAS's top-15 diagonal span range widened from "almost universally 2600+ days" to a healthy 918-2813 day mix. 155 tests passing.
19. **Integrated volume weighting into scoring** (the one confirmed gap from an external best-practices checklist — everything else on it, clustering-into-zones/RANSAC-for-diagonals/angle-duration-limits/zones-not-lines/breakout-role-flip, was already covered): `scoring._volume_factor(volume_ratio)` — neutral at average volume or when missing, capped 1.3x boost at 2x+ volume, floored 0.7x penalty at zero volume — applied in `_event_quality_score` (so both `touch_quality` and `role_reversal` benefit) and in `_resilience`. Also fixed a related small bug found while in this code: `events._merge_adjacent` kept whichever merged event's volume was chronologically later, not the max, inconsistent with how `penetration_atr`/`reaction_atr` already merge. 158 tests passing. Also reviewed (not changed): wick-fake vs. body-fake resilience credit may be inverted from what real-world evidence-strength would suggest — flagged as a values judgment, not fixed unilaterally, see design notes.
20. **Fixed the "hovering" bug — the biggest scoring-architecture finding this round**: user kept reporting diagonal lines that looked disconnected from real price across multiple review rounds even after the dedup and event/geometry fixes above. Root cause: every line in a real AAPL top-15 had `resilience=1.000` and `role_reversal=1.000` (trivially saturated over 6-8 years of real break/reclaim cycles), while `duration_density` correctly detected the hovering (0.19-0.53) but its additive weight couldn't suppress a line maxed on three other axes — the same structural bug as the original `proximity` fix. Fixed by splitting `duration_density` into `_duration_score` (kept additive, span-length only) and a new `_in_play_fraction` promoted to a second multiplicative gate (`in_play_gate`, alongside `relevance_gate`). Verified: AAPL's top score dropped 0.368 → 0.202 with real re-ranking; applies uniformly to horizontal too. Also surfaced the previously-invisible `relevance_gate`/`in_play_gate`/`diagonal_penalty` in the chart's hover text. 160 tests passing.
21. **Fixed a diagonal merge pulling a survivor's displayed `first_touch` back past its own fitted geometry**: found from a plain user question ("where is this starting from?") about a single visible AAPL diagonal line. `lifecycle._absorb` extended `first_touch` backward to an absorbed candidate's earlier value for *all* merges, valid for horizontal (constant zone bounds) but not diagonal (survivor keeps its own slope/intercept through a merge, never refit to the earlier period) — a real AAPL line had `first_touch` pulled back to 2018-09 while all 5 of its own defining pivots were 2020-07 through 2026-02, rendering a ~30%-off fitted price at the extended start date. Fixed: `first_touch` extension is now horizontal-only. Verified on a fresh AAPL run: the top diagonal line's `first_touch` is now exactly its own earliest pivot (2020-07-13), fitted price within 1.6% of the real close that day. 161 tests passing.
