- **Anchored volume profile (Done #56) — open follow-ups.** (a) **Intraday data:** `bars_1h` is empty, so profiles spread each *daily* bar's volume evenly across its high-low range. `validate_intraday.py` measured this against Yahoo's free ~2-year hourly window: fine for VAH/VAL and median POC on 20+ bar anchors (~0.2-0.6 ATR), but ~22% of 60+ bar anchors put the POC on a different volume node than hourly does, and <20-bar anchors are essentially undetermined. Worth a paid intraday source (Polygon/Databento/Alpaca minute history back to ~2010) only if a downstream use actually depends on the POC of short or bimodal profiles; `build_profile` already accepts finer bars, so filling `bars_1h` is the only change needed. (b) **Default `row_scale`:** linear (TradingView's convention) degenerates on anchors spanning huge split-adjusted ranges (GEVO's 2011 ATH puts 100% of volume in one row); `log` fixes it. Open question whether log should become the default, or be auto-selected past some high/low ratio. (c) Share volume across decades is split-adjusted, so multi-decade ATL profiles are dominated by the low-price era (AAPL 1982 ATL POC $1.76) — a dollar-volume mode would be the alternative. (d) Not built: HVN/LVN (high/low-volume node) detection, naked-POC tracking, price interaction with POC/VA over time (analogous to avwap's `n_crosses`/touch reaction), and a full-universe run (same open bulk-detection item as the modules above).
