22. **Root-caused and fixed the real bug behind what first looked like a "confirmed by design" case**: a well-fit (`fit_rms=0.068`), genuinely real PAAS descending resistance with an ordinary ~3-year dormant gap before a decisive 2024 breakout scored 0.032 even evaluated right as it broke — not because of `relevance_gate` (which was fine, 0.70), but because `in_play_gate` averages over a line's *entire* event history, so the old dead years stayed baked in regardless of "now." 26. **Fixed BREAK markers reading as floating, disconnected from any candle**: user flagged this on a real, dense AAPL chart. ## In progress / open questions

- **`in_play_gate` is one flat fraction averaged over a line's whole regime, so it can't tell "scattered near-misses" from "one solid multi-month block genuinely on price, one solid multi-month block genuinely nowhere near it"** — both can average to the same number, but the second reads as an obviously floating segment when you look at that specific stretch. Confirmed on real AAPL data: several lines with a reasonable-looking aggregate `in_play_gate` (0.4-0.8) had local in-play fractions as low as 0-19% within a specific multi-month window, chronically 5-20 ATR from price there. Same structural failure shape as the original "hovering" bug and the chop-saturation fix — an aggregate can't suppress a *local* problem it dilutes across the average. Not yet fixed; likely needs something like a max-contiguous-out-of-play penalty, not just the existing flat fraction. Deferred at the user's direction to first fix the (separate, now-fixed, see Done #26) marker-positioning issue.

- **Weekly-bar calibration, proposed but not started**: user's idea — run detection against weekly-resampled bars (already available, `bars_1w` table) instead of/alongside daily, on the theory that aggregating 5 daily candles into one weekly candle should naturally convert a lot of what registers as a same-day BODY_FAKE at daily resolution (a brief intraday/1-day close beyond the zone that reclaims) into a WICK_FAKE on the weekly candle instead — real noise reduction, not a hack, since it's a direct consequence of the coarser aggregation rather than a new rule. Likely secondary benefit: far fewer pivots over the same multi-year window should mean less RANSAC over-fitting into near-duplicate diagonals (related to Done #28/#29's clutter findings). Treat as a separate, deliberately-scoped effort, not a quick toggle.
