## In progress / open questions

- **`in_play_gate` is one flat fraction averaged over a line's whole regime, so it can't tell "scattered near-misses" from "one solid multi-month block genuinely on price, one solid multi-month block genuinely nowhere near it"** — both can average to the same number, but the second reads as an obviously floating segment when you look at that specific stretch. Confirmed on real AAPL data: several lines with a reasonable-looking aggregate `in_play_gate` (0.4-0.8) had local in-play fractions as low as 0-19% within a specific multi-month window, chronically 5-20 ATR from price there. Same structural failure shape as the original "hovering" bug and the chop-saturation fix — an aggregate can't suppress a *local* problem it dilutes across the average. Not yet fixed; likely needs something like a max-contiguous-out-of-play penalty, not just the existing flat fraction. Deferred at the user's direction to first fix the (separate, now-fixed, see Done #26) marker-positioning issue.
- Whether/how to source quarterly financials — still gated behind an undecided Polygon paid tier.
- **Data quality gap found via the sr_lines diagonal review, unrelated to sr_lines itself**: T's 2023-01-24 daily bar has `high=17.81` against `open=15.69`/`close=15.85` — an ~13% intraday spike that fully reverses same-day. Not caught by the existing validation gate (`data.py`'s hard checks only verify internal OHLC consistency; the soft day-over-day jump check only looks at close-to-close continuity, not intraday range plausibility). Not yet investigated further — worth checking whether this is an isolated bad print or a broader gap (e.g. an intraday-range-vs-ATR sanity check, similar in spirit to the existing day-over-day close check).
- **`sr_lines` milestone 5, diagonal real-chart review still in progress**: many real rounds of bugs found and fixed so far (dedup logic, event/geometry consistency, long-history duration bias, the hovering/in_play_gate architecture fix, first_touch-on-merge, near-flat/horizontal duplication, cap starvation — see Done #16-29); overall visual quality is much improved but still worth further passes as more tickers get reviewed. Also open: whether `max_diagonal_slope_atr_per_bar`'s log-slope interpretation for the *rejection* cap is the right one, and why raising `--dedup-threshold` has a much weaker effect on diagonal clutter than it does on horizontal. Two open calibration questions carried forward: `resilience`'s cap (1.0) can still be hit by a zone with enough *recent* events even after the time-decay fix (now applied, see Done #22), and whether wick-fake vs. body-fake resilience credit is inverted from what real-world evidence-strength suggests — see `docs/sr_lines_design_notes.md`.
- **Remaining half of the penetration-depth/volume "erosion" signal idea, not yet built**: the volume half is now resolved (see Done). The penetration-depth *trend* half — is a level being tested with deepening (weakening) or shallowing (strengthening) penetration over time — is still open. See `docs/sr_lines_design_notes.md` for the proposed trend-based approach, deferred to the weight-tuning pass.
- **Weekly-bar calibration, proposed but not started**: user's idea — run detection against weekly-resampled bars (already available, `bars_1w` table) instead of/alongside daily, on the theory that aggregating 5 daily candles into one weekly candle should naturally convert a lot of what registers as a same-day BODY_FAKE at daily resolution (a brief intraday/1-day close beyond the zone that reclaims) into a WICK_FAKE on the weekly candle instead — real noise reduction, not a hack, since it's a direct consequence of the coarser aggregation rather than a new rule. Likely secondary benefit: far fewer pivots over the same multi-year window should mean less RANSAC over-fitting into near-duplicate diagonals (related to Done #28/#29's clutter findings). Real cost, not yet scoped: every daily-bar-unit threshold (`fakeout_reclaim_bars`, `touch_reaction_window_bars`, ATR windows, `diagonal_min_pivot_separation_bars`) is tuned in daily-bar units and would need its own weekly calibration pass, not a blind resample of the same config; marker/event timing precision also gets coarser (events snap to week boundaries), a real regression right after Done #26's crossing-bar precision fix. Treat as a separate, deliberately-scoped effort, not a quick toggle.
