## Signals (gaps, divergences, fibonacci, avwap, volume profile)

- **Anchored volume profile (Done #56) — open follow-ups.** (a) **Intraday data:** `bars_1h` is empty, so profiles spread each *daily* bar's volume evenly across its high-low range. `validate_intraday.py` measured this against Yahoo's free ~2-year hourly window: fine for VAH/VAL and median POC on 20+ bar anchors (~0.2-0.6 ATR), but ~22% of 60+ bar anchors put the POC on a different volume node than hourly does, and <20-bar anchors are essentially undetermined.
