## 2026-09-08

- **Robustness check on the 2018-2021 skew/kurtosis pattern** (2026-09-07 finding above): recomputed era skew/kurtosis excluding 2018Q4 (2018-10-01 → 2018-12-31) and the COVID crash (2020-02-01 → 2020-04-30) — only 4.1% of panel rows. **Does not survive** for `dist_pct_sma_50` (skew flips sign: -0.85 → +0.66) or `dist_z_sma_50` (-0.74 → -0.20, moves to the *middle* of the three eras, no longer the extreme one). `dist_atr_sma_50` never showed the pattern to begin with (-0.16 vs. -0.16/-0.12 baselines — essentially flat across eras). Only `slope_log_21_sma_50` keeps a residual left skew after exclusion (-1.40 → -1.02 vs. ~-0.44 baselines), and even that isn't fully crash-free — a 21-day lookback bleeds into the days just after the exclusion window. EXPLORATORY. **Conclusion: the original era-dependence finding was mostly a crash-driven artifact concentrated in ~4% of the sample, not a broad distributional shift across 2018-2021.** Informs M4's design: starts unconditional, era/regime enters as a sensitivity facet (plateau/subperiod check), not a core requirement.
