=== ITEM 28
QUESTION: how does the validation harness split train/test for the combined model?
CHUNK:
The gate caught one tie: two windows of cent prices can hold the same values, so their widths
or vols tie exactly. Pandas' running-sum rolling std breaks the tie in float after a later
split or dividend, and a trailing percentile rank would then depend on it. Values are rounded
to 9 decimals before ranking; the same goes for the cross-sectionally ranked
`rel_volume_20` and the sector returns, where two names can tie exactly. The cache's panel
source reads every name that was a member of the universe's indices in the window (recorded
as `panel_membership_indices`); the per-ticker sources are still computed on the eligible names only.

**Ties.** Comparisons between price moves or levels use a tie tolerance (1e-9 × close):
ADX's "which move is larger", the ribbon's "slope > 0" and the stack's ordering. Quoted
(cent-rounded) prices tie exactly, and a later split or dividend breaks such ties in
floating point. A strict comparison would then make the value at *t* depend on corporate
actions after *t*. The real-bar smoke run caught it on ADX (Tiingo's cent prices; up to 3.9
ADX points). The gate's synthetic bars are now cent-rounded, so the synthetic gate catches
this class too.

`features/cache.py` builds one cache of every registered model column for a universe's
eligible rows, ranked per date over those rows (`python -m src.models.cli build-features`).

=== ITEM 30
QUESTION: Do we have any financial data from companies' earning reports?

## Why

- **Earnings dates are the largest known gap for the model.** An earnings gap jumps
  straight through both barriers of a triple-barrier label, and today nothing can flag
  "earnings inside the horizon" (`docs/modeling/IDEAS.md`, "Earnings dates" in §3 and §7).
- **The model only sees prices.** Growth, surprise and valuation are an independent
  source of information: the classic academic effects (post-earnings drift, value,
  quality) come from here, not from charts.
- **The raw data is already on disk and nearly unused.** `data/raw/sec/submissions.zip`
  (~1.6 GB, every filer's filing index) and `data/raw/sec/companyfacts.zip` (~1.4 GB,
  every XBRL fact). Today only share counts are read from them (Done #60).

=== ITEM 35
QUESTION: Do we consider Sharpe value in our model and/or analysis?
CHUNK:
## Ground rules

- **Point-in-time by filing time.** Every value is available only from its EDGAR
  acceptance timestamp onward. A filing accepted after the close is usable at the
  next day's open, never the same bar.
- **First-reported values, not restated ones.** Companies revise past numbers in later
  filings; using the revised value is look-ahead. Keep each value as it was first filed,
  and keep later revisions as separate rows if needed.
- **Delisted companies stay in.** EDGAR covers them; drop none.
- **Holdout and trial rules from the modeling thread apply** to anything that reaches a
  model or a test (`docs/modeling/VALIDATION_HARNESS.md`).
